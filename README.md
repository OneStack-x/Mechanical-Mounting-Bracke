# Mechanical Mounting Bracket – 3D CAD Design

## Project Overview
This project presents the 3D CAD design of a mechanical mounting bracket featuring a central cylindrical boss, multiple mounting holes, and integrated supporting geometry.

The component was modeled to demonstrate mechanical design skills, geometric modeling, and CAD engineering techniques.

## Design Features
- Upper mounting plate with two holes.
- Central cylindrical boss with a through-hole.
- Lower mounting section with an additional mounting hole.
- Curved surfaces and integrated structural geometry.
- Multiple features combined into a single mechanical component.

## Engineering & CAD Skills
- 3D Part Modeling
- Sketching and Geometric Constraints
- Pad / Extrusion Operations
- Pocket / Cut Operations
- Fillet and Edge Treatments
- Hole Features
- Mechanical Component Design

## Software
-  CATIA V5

- `3D_Model/` – Original CAD model files.

## Learning Objectives
- Improve mechanical part modeling skills.
- Understand the relationship between sketches and 3D features.
- Practice designing mounting and supporting components.
- Develop a professional mechanical engineering portfolio.
